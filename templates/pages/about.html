How it works

Every hour we read around 40 news feeds: national papers, UK tech titles, a few global tech sites and official sources such as Ofcom, the CMA and the NCSC. Articles about the same event are grouped into one story.

Each story is then ranked by how many outlets are covering it. National outlets count for more than specialist ones, a story needs a clear UK angle to appear, and older stories slowly sink. Nobody picks the order by hand.

Every headline links straight to the publisher that wrote it. We don't copy articles.

The "So what" lines

The short "So what" line under a story is written by AI from the coverage. It's there to save you a click, and like any summary it can be wrong. The publisher's article is always the source to trust.
