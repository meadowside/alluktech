What this site collects

{% if site.ga_id %}

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, Google Analytics sets cookies that count visits and show which pages and stories get read. Google sees your IP address and basic details about your browser and device. We see only totals. If you click No thanks, Analytics never loads. You can change your mind any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

There are no tracking pixels and no advertising cookies.

{% else %}

Nothing that identifies you, unless you use the contact form. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising cookies.

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice (and your cookie choice) is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.
